A quick safety note

FixFlow provides general guidance and cannot inspect your device. Don't open power supplies, remove internal parts, disable security tools, or delete unfamiliar system files. If you suspect malware, data loss, overheating, or electrical damage, stop and ask a trusted technician or IT administrator.
